# UI01 Deep-sea UI retheme

Base NeoForge 1.21.1 (`F:\Java\Abyssia-NeoForge`), then port to Forge 1.20.1 (`F:\Java\Abyssia`).
Style = the guide book frame (`gui/guide/book_bg.png`): navy riveted metal, steel brackets, 1px light-blue bevel, glowing cyan gems, small coral/bubble ornaments.

## Design sources (ChatGPT, traced) — `tools/ui_kit/src/`
Design origin; never overwrite.
- Sheet A, 32x32 each: `ui01a_corner` (top-left frame corner: steel bracket + cyan gem + coral), `ui01a_edge` (top frame band in rows ~0-8 with centre gem, rest interior), `ui01a_fill` (interior, coral/bubbles in two corners), `ui01a_slot`, `ui01a_button`, `ui01a_button_hover`.
- Sheet B: `ui01b_menu_bg` 16x16 seamless, `ui01b_checkbox` 20x20 (ticked), `ui01b_globe` 14x14, `ui01b_lock_closed` 10x13, `ui01b_lock_open` 11x13, `ui01b_envelope` 15x11 (alpha 0 = transparent).
- Palette (sheet A): abyss `021123`, navy_deep `021c38`, navy `0b2a49`, navy_mid `133c62`, blue `175482`, blue_light `257dae`, steel_blue `528fbb`, pale_blue `6eafd3`, steel_dark `476c8c`, steel `7c9ab2`, steel_light `a9c0d0`, highlight `c1f3f8`, cyan_deep `12a6da`, cyan `43bcdc`, cyan_glow `58eef9`, cyan_pale `87ddf8`.

## Rules
- Final PNGs come only from generators in `tools/` (never hand-edit outputs). Flat palette colours, 1px bevel, no anti-aliasing. Functional colours (amber, flame, energy) may stay.
- Mojang textures: never copy pixels and never read vanilla files at generation time. Sizes/UV regions are hard-coded constants (vanilla files may be inspected while writing the code).
- Keep every layout coordinate unchanged (slots, arrows, gauges; `industrial_gui.py` mirrors `GuiLayout.java`).
- Unrelated uncommitted files exist in both trees (other work): do not touch them. Subagents do not run gradle.

## Part 1 — ui_kit + Abyssia textures
1. `tools/ui_kit.py`: palette + shared helpers: panel `frame` (corner piece cut 1:1 from the corner tile and mirrored for the other corners, edge band from the edge tile, transposed for vertical edges, interior from a calm part of the fill tile, optional coral/bubble ornament in free corners), `slot` 18x18 recessed (1px dark top-left, light bottom-right, navy inside), `button(state)` nine-sliced from the button tiles (`disabled` = darker, no cyan), `gem`, `bevel`. No resampling of pixel art other than integer crops.
2. `industrial_gui.py`, `locker_gui.py`: use ui_kit. Same outputs, same layout.
3. `guide_gui.py`: add a target filter (e.g. `--only buttons`) and regenerate only `gui/guide/buttons.png` (60x56: rows TOC, PREV, NEXT, CLOSE every 14px; columns normal, hover, disabled every 20px; glyphs ≡ < > × centred). Do not regenerate book_bg, pages or icons.
4. Themed backgrounds for screens that currently draw vanilla textures:
   - `abyssia:textures/gui/wall_workbench.png` (176x166): 3x3 grid at (30,17) step 18, result (124,35) as a 26x26 big slot, arrow between them, energy gauge frame at (155,17) 12x32 (frame 1px outside), player inventory at (8,84), hotbar at (8,142). `WallWorkbenchScreen` uses it, drops its fill-drawn slot frames, and uses `UiTheme` gauge colours.
   - `abyssia:textures/gui/submarine_upgrade.png` (176x143): title bar, upgrade slot row at (44,30) step 18 (count from `SubmarineUpgradeMenu`), inventory at y=61, hotbar at y=119. `SubmarineUpgradeScreen` draws it with one blit.
