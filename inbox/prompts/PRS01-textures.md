# PRS01 pressure gear icons (ChatGPT, 16x16 item icons, transparent background)

Current files are hue/brightness-shifted placeholders (tools/placeholder_items.py); replace them with real art and lock.
Style: same as dive_tank / diving_suit_leggings / abyssal_flippers icons, but the "hadal pressure" look from
pressure_diver_helmet: dark gunmetal with a purple-blue cast (#1A1C2A #2C3050 #464C74 #6A6F90), bolted plates, pale rivets (#A3A8CC).
Flat few-colour pixel art, 1 px dark outline, one item per image, no text.

1. `pressure_dive_tank` - back-mounted dive tank (chestpiece), thick gunmetal cylinders, bolted bands, small valve.
2. `pressure_suit_leggings` - sealed diving suit leggings, heavy plated knees with bolts.
3. `pressure_flippers` - reinforced flippers, dark plated top with bolted straps.

Output: textures/item/<id>.png (replace the placeholders), then lock in tools/texture_locks.
