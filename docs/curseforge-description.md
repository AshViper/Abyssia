# Abyssia

**Dive past the bedrock. The real deep sea is waiting.**

Abyssia adds a whole new layer of ocean beneath the world: a vast, dark, pressurised abyss
populated by more than 50 real deep-sea species. Find a fissure in the ocean floor, follow it down,
and keep going. There are no portals and no loading screens, just the long swim into the dark.

---

## 🌊 The Deep Layer

- A continuous deep-ocean layer generated **below the vanilla bedrock band** (Y -64 to -368).
- Reach it through **deep fissures** that split the ocean floor. You descend the whole way yourself, with no teleport.
- The default world type keeps vanilla terrain on the surface. Choose the optional **Abyssia Ocean** world type for an all-ocean world.
- **17 deep-sea biomes**, including Twilight Reef, Abyssal Forest, Deep Crystal Fields, Volcanic Deep,
  Thermal Vents, Brine Lakes, Glow Gardens, Frost Abyss, Sunken Ruins, Bone Graveyard, Abyssal Trench and the Hadal Zone.
- Biomes form **large, readable regions** instead of tiny patches, so every expedition feels like a journey.
- Huge caverns, gas pockets, rifts and a full family of deep stones, ores and building blocks.
- **Seabed structures** that fit each biome: rock formations, vent chimneys, ruins and more.

## 🐟 Real Deep-Sea Life

Every creature is based on a **real species** and lives at depths that match its real range.

- **Anglerfish, Gulper Eel, Barreleye, Fangtooth, Viperfish, Black Dragonfish, Blobfish, Tripod Fish**
- **Goblin Shark, Frilled Shark, Pacific Sleeper Shark, Cookiecutter Shark, Bluntnose Sixgill Shark**
- **Giant Squid, Vampire Squid, Firefly Squid, Bigfin Squid, Dumbo Octopus**
- **Giant Isopod, Japanese Spider Crab, Supergiant Amphipod, Sea Pig, Giant Sea Spider**
- **Hydrothermal vent communities**: Tubeworms, Yunohana Crabs, Squat Lobsters, Scaly-foot Snails, Vent Eelpouts and more cluster around the vents.
- **Drifting jellies** such as the Atolla Jelly, Helmet Jelly, Giant Phantom Jelly and Deepstaria pulse and glide with the currents.
- …and many more, including the Mariana Snailfish, Oarfish, Sea Lily and Venus' Flower Basket.

Creatures have their own behaviours. They flee and hunt, they move between depths, and their
**bioluminescence** glows in the darkness. Some of them can be harvested for food or crafting materials.

Already using other ocean mods? Abyssia can **detect their sea creatures automatically** and let them spawn at suitable depths (configurable).

## 🌀 A Living Ocean

- **Ocean currents** push players, mobs, items and boats.
- **Natural current streams** cross the sea floor and are visible as streaks of particles.
- **Marine snow**, depth fog and lighting that change as you go deeper.
- A custom **deep-sea title screen panorama**.

## 🌿 Resources & Crafting

- **Deep-sea resource plants** that ripen and regrow, giving fibres, shells and other materials.
- New ores and minerals, each with its own raw, crystal and powder forms.
- Fish, eel and shark meat can be eaten and cooked. Jelly tentacles are useful crafting materials.

## 🤿 Diving Gear

- An **entry diving set** made with vanilla materials, which refills your air as you explore the shallows.
- Advanced **deep-sea gear** for the crushing depths below.

## ⚡ Industry Powered by the Abyss

- **Thermal-vent generators** turn the heat of hydrothermal vents into **Forge Energy (FE)** (NeoForge: FE via the NeoForge energy capability).
- Machines such as crushers, smelters and alloy furnaces, plus batteries and an **energy cable network**.
- Compatible with other FE mods: Abyssia cables can deliver power to their machines.
- Electric drills and cutters, plus a propulsion screw that pulls you through the water.

## 🏠 Underwater Bases

- **Habitat modules**: dry, air-filled rooms that you place on the sea floor. Their hatches connect to each other automatically.
- Base equipment: lockers, a wall workbench that also charges tools, a 3D constructor and a **scan room**.
- **Waypoint beacons** that mark places on your HUD so you can always find your way home.

## 📋 Requirements & Compatibility

- **Minecraft 1.20.1**, **Forge 47.x**  (this file describes the Forge 1.20.1 build; the NeoForge 1.21.1 port targets Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.x)
- **New worlds only.** The deep layer changes world height, so Abyssia cannot be added to an existing world.
- Works with **Embeddium** and **Oculus/Iris shader packs**.
- **JEI** is optional and adds information pages for materials.
- Most features can be adjusted in the config files, including spawn rates, currents and visual effects.
